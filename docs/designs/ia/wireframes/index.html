Minutes IA wireframes

Low-fidelity screens for the proposed information architecture. Real HeroUI components, Bricolage Grotesque, at the app's default 1120×720 window. Structure, not final visuals.

01 · Main window

Meeting open as a pane, Assistant beside it, Record in the top bar, This week pinned.

02 · Recording

The Record menu, the in-place recording control, one banner at a time.

03 · This week

The fixed Weekly view, computed from decisions, action items and commitments.

04 · Settings

Grouped by task, one row pattern, real switches, plain language.

05 · Activity

Processing, recovery and setup checks in one drawer, ordered by urgency.

06 · Draft beside its meeting

Drafts tab in the meeting, editor alongside, refine through the Assistant.

07 · First run

Setup in the main pane: model, permissions, a test recording.

08 · Tray and palette

State-aware tray menu, palette results, same names everywhere.

Decisions these screens follow

  • Meeting is the saved thing; Memo is a kind of meeting, hidden from the list unless chosen.
  • The AI chat is the Assistant. Drafts belong to their meeting.
  • A meeting opens as a pane. This week is a fixed view.
  • Coach sits with recording. Dictation is a utility, not a top-level area.

Full reasoning: ../00-current-state.md and ../04-proposed-structure.md.
