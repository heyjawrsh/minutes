9:02 Follow up on SSO

  1. The Record menu names each kind of recording plainly and shows its shortcut. "Live" stops being a separate mode: it is a meeting with a live transcript. Audio source and microphone move here from the footer, next to the action they affect.
  2. While recording, the Record button becomes the recording control in place: time, level, Add note, Coach, Stop. No separate recording bar, live bar, or footer state.
  3. Capture state uses the accent (capture) color; red stays reserved for errors.
  4. A recording is a meeting from the first second: it appears under "Now" in the list and opens in the meeting pane with Live transcript and Notes tabs. After Stop, the same pane becomes Overview / Transcript / Drafts.
  5. One banner at a time, by priority (blocking errors › capture problems › call detected › upcoming › info). "+1 more" opens the rest in Activity.
